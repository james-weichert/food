Schnitzel describes a broad category of meat cutlets in German cooking, most often veal or pork. Austrian Wiener schnitzel is perhaps the most recognizable, and in my opinion the most delicious. I make my version by **thinly pounding out a pork loin, breading with standard breadcrumbs, and shallow frying**. The classic way of serving Wiener schnitzel is with lemon and fries, although I usually serve my schnitzel with spätzle (egg noodles).
