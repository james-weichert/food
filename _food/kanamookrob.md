One of my recent favorites at Bangrak Market has been kana moo grob, a stir fry of crispy pork belly and Chinese broccoli (gai lan). The preparation (including frying the pork belly) demands a good amount of planning and coordination, but the dish itself is relatively simple; fry a **base of garlic and Thai chili before adding sliced Chinese broccoli, oyster sauce, soy sauce, fish sauce, and sugar.** Wait until the very end to add the fried pork belly to prevent it from losing its crispiness.
