Literally translating to "chile water", aguachile is a regional Mexican seafood dish that I think is easier to pull off at home than Peruvian ceviche. **Marinate raw shrimp and red onion with a lot of lime juice and create the sauce from cilantro, chile (like serrano peppers), and lime**. Once the shrimp turns pink, pour the sauce over the shrimp and add cucumber and avocado. You can air fry corn tortillas to make tostadas.
