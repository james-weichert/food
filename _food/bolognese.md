Pasta with Bolognese ragu has always been a staple in my kitchen, but taking the final step of making my own pasta always seemed too daunting. On a recent trip to Bologna, I overcame my pasta-making fears and learned how to make tagliatelle pasta by hand. With a little guidance (from my cooking class instructor or an Italian grandmother on YouTube) and enough patience, it's actually not that hard to make. Combine **one egg for every 100 grams of pasta flour**. Knead until the dough is combined and springy before resting for 30 minutes. Then, carefully roll out the dough until it is as thin as possible and cut into half-inch strips before cooking.
